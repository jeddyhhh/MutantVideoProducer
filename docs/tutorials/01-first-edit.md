# 1. Your First Edit

You learn: playing, trimming, splitting, moving events, crossfades, editing a title, undo, saving
and rendering.

**Example project:** [first-edit](../../data/examples/first-edit.mvp). Click the link, or in Mutant Video Producer
choose **Help > Tutorials > Your First Edit** and click it there.

## What's in it

Three tracks, 14 seconds:

- **Titles**: *My First Edit*, fading in and out, and a lower third over the stars.
- **Pictures**: three animated backgrounds. *Plasma* and *Aurora* overlap by a second, so they
  crossfade. *Aurora* and *Starfield* just touch, so they cut.
- **Music**, fading out at the end.

## 1. Play it

1. Press **Space** to play. Press it again to stop: the cursor goes back to where you started.
2. Press **Enter** to play, and **Enter** again to pause where you are.
3. Click on the ruler to move the cursor. Drag along the ruler to scrub: you hear the music under the cursor.
4. Press **L** to play, **L** again for double speed, **K** to stop, **J** to go backwards.
5. Press **Ctrl+0** to fit the whole project in view. **↑** and **↓** zoom.

## 2. Trim and split

1. Drag the **right edge** of the *Starfield* event to the left to make it shorter.
2. Put the cursor in the middle of the *Plasma* event and click the event to select it.
3. Press **S** to split it in two.
4. Click the second half and press **Del** to delete it. A gap is left.
5. Press **Ctrl+Z** twice to undo both.

## 3. Make a crossfade

1. Drag the *Starfield* event to the left so it overlaps the end of *Aurora* by about a second.
   The overlap is drawn as a crossfade.
2. Play across it.
3. Drag the small handle in the top corner of the *Plasma* event's start to the right: it now fades
   in from black.

## 4. Change the title

1. Click the *My First Edit* event. Its settings open in the **Properties** tab at the bottom.
2. Change the **Text** to your own title.
3. Change the **Font**, **Size** and **Text color**.
4. On the preview, drag the title to move it.

## 5. Add an effect

1. Open the **Video FX** tab (top left).
2. Drag **Glow** (in *Blur & Sharpen*) onto the *Aurora* event.
3. Its controls appear in the device chain. Drag the **Intensity** knob up or down.

## 6. Undo and History

1. Open the **History** tab. Every step you took is listed.
2. Click a step near the top to go back to it, then the last step to come forward again.

## 7. Save and render

1. Press **Ctrl+S**. The project is your copy in `Documents\Mutant Video Producer Examples`.
2. Press **Ctrl+R** (**File > Render As**).
3. Type *youtube* in the search box and choose **YouTube 1080p**.
4. Choose where to save the file and click **Render**.
